Executive Summary & Research History

This project tested common market timing claims across 25+ years of daily data (1998–Present):

  • 3-Month Momentum Rotation: Failed. Rank autocorrelation was -0.021 with a 32.4% hit-rate (below random 33.3%).

Daily Hidden Markov Model (HMM) Regimes

At the top of the Android application, a daily 3-state Gaussian Hidden Markov Model categorizes the broader market environment into three regimes:

🟢 Low Volatility / Slow Grind Up

Steady compounding regime. Cross-sectional dispersion is orderly. Default 100% invested in the rebalancing basket.

🟡 Choppy / Sideways

Mean-reverting, oscillating environment. Optimal regime for Shannon's Demon to aggressively harvest volatility across sector rotations.

🔴 High Volatility / Crash

High-stress turbulent shock. Alerts the investor to severe market distress and provides guidance for optional 50% equity / 50% cash preservation.

Risk Profiles: Shock Absorber vs Wealth Compounder

Shock Absorber (5% Band)

Rebalances whenever any sector drifts $\pm 5.0\%$ from the 9.09% target. Maximizes volatility capture and provides the greatest drawdown reduction during crashes.

Wealth Compounder (10% Band)

Rebalances only when drift reaches $\pm 10.0\%$. Gives strong winners more room to run, minimizing turnover transaction friction and maximizing capital compounding.
